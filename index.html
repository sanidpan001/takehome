What is In-Hand Salary Calculator? Worldwide Guide 2026

In-Hand Salary or Take-Home Salary is the actual amount you receive in your bank account after all deductions like PF, Income Tax, Gratuity, Professional Tax. CTC (Cost to Company) is not your salary - it includes company contributions too. Our TakeHome In-Hand Salary Calculator is 100% free, unlimited, no watermark, works worldwide for India, USA, UK, Dubai, Canada. It calculates exact monthly and yearly in-hand from CTC with New Regime 2026 and Old Regime.

For example, if your CTC is Rs 12 Lakh per annum in India, your in-hand is NOT Rs 1 Lakh per month. After 12% PF (Rs 57,600), Gratuity (Rs 23k), and Income Tax under new regime (Rs 42k), your yearly in-hand is Rs 10.14L, monthly Rs 84,550. That's 84.5% of CTC. Many freshers get confused between CTC and in-hand. This calculator clears that.

How In-Hand Salary Calculator Works? Formula

Formula India: Gross = CTC - Employer PF (12% of Basic) - Gratuity (4.81% of Basic). Basic = CTC * Basic%. HRA = Basic * HRA%. Special Allowance = Gross - Basic - HRA. Employee PF = 12% of Basic. Taxable Income = Gross - Standard Deduction (Rs 75,000 new regime) - 80C (if old). Income Tax calculated as per slab: New Regime 2026: 0-4L 0%, 4-8L 5%, 8-12L 10%, 12-16L 15%, 16-20L 20%, 20-24L 25%, 24L+ 30%. Then In-Hand Yearly = Gross - Employee PF - Income Tax - Professional Tax (Rs 2400). Monthly = Yearly/12.

For USA: In-hand = Gross - Federal Tax (22% avg) - State Tax (5%) - Social Security (6.2%) - Medicare (1.45%). For UK: In-hand = Gross - Income Tax (20% slab) - NI (8%). For Dubai: No tax, In-hand = CTC itself. Our calculator auto-switches formula based on country tab.

Tips to Increase In-Hand Salary from Same CTC

1. Choose New Regime if income < Rs 15L - you save tax without investments. 2. Increase Basic? No, keep Basic 40% only to reduce PF deduction and increase in-hand. 3. Claim HRA exemption: If rent is Rs 25k in metro and basic Rs 40k, HRA exemption = min(HRA received, Rent-10% basic, 50% basic) = Rs 21k/month tax-free. 4. Use 80C for old regime: PPF, ELSS, Life Insurance Rs 1.5L reduces tax. 5. Ask company for tax-free components: Food coupons (Rs 2200/month tax-free), Fuel reimbursement, NPS contribution (Rs 50k extra deduction).

For USA remote workers: Ask for contractor role - you get full CTC without PF, but pay self-employment tax 15.3%. For Dubai: Zero income tax, so Rs 12L CTC = Rs 12L in-hand, but cost of living high.
